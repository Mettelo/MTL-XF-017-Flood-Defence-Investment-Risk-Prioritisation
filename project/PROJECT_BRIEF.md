# Project Brief

## Client Commission
The authority has more maintenance and resilience needs than available capital. Programme teams cannot consistently compare scheme priority, funding allocation, delivery status and evidence of risk reduction across one governed view.

> **Decision to support:** Which flood-defence schemes or asset interventions should receive the earliest investment attention, and how should leadership balance risk, delivery readiness, funding and evidence limitations?

## Primary Dataset
**Environment Agency Flood and Coastal Erosion Risk Management Investment Programme data**  
Publisher: Environment Agency  
Official source: https://environment.data.gov.uk/asset-management/doc/about

## Delivery Lifecycle
**Discovery → Requirements → Governance → Acquisition → Engineering → Data Quality → Analysis → Decision Framework → BI/Product → Assurance/UAT → Executive Recommendation → Handover**

## Cross-Functional Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Define investment decision, stakeholder needs, constraints and acceptance criteria. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Govern scheme identifiers, funding fields, ownership, definitions and lineage. |
| Data Engineer | Build reproducible ingestion of programme/asset data. |
| Flood Risk/Data Analyst | Analyse investment patterns, scheme portfolio and delivery pressure. |
| GIS Analyst | Assess spatial distribution and regional concentration appropriately. |
| BI Developer | Build capital-programme and prioritisation views. |
| Investment/Risk Analyst | Challenge decision criteria, materiality and evidence gaps. |

## Business Questions
1. What is the current baseline and where is material variation concentrated?
2. Which data-quality, coverage or definition issues could materially alter the decision?
3. Which segments, areas, assets, recipients or corridors require deeper investigation?
4. Which indicators are suitable for prioritisation, and which should not be used that way?
5. What intervention choices are genuinely supported by evidence?
6. What should management monitor after handover?

## Success Criteria
Traceable requirements; governed definitions/lineage/quality; reproducible transformations; defensible scheme portfolio, funding allocation, delivery profile, geographic concentration, investment prioritisation and portfolio risk; transparent decision logic; useful BI/product; UAT evidence; executive recommendation; maintainable handover; visible collaborative contribution.

## Panel Challenge
The review panel may challenge assumptions, denominators, definitions, data quality, thresholds, models, spatial interpretation, governance, recommendations and maintainability.
